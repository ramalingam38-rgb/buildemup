### Phase 4 — S7b SPEC v0.4 DRAFT (round 3 critique)

Round 3 surfaced 1 item — but it was a real one: stuck rows
(fallback_fired = 0 AND attempt_count >= 3) had operational
visibility via /health but NO recovery path other than direct DB
surgery. This contradicts S7b's API-surface-as-self-service model.

P35 added: admin reset endpoint POST /admin/scheduler/reset with
single-row, idempotent, atomic, audited semantics. Same auth as
the tick endpoint. Three outcomes: RESET / ALREADY_FIRED / NOT_FOUND.

This was the right call to flip my round 2 "ready to lock"
recommendation. The reviewer's framing as a "completeness gap, not
architectural flaw" was accurate; adding the recovery endpoint made
P33 more coherent (detection + action) rather than adding rules on
top of rules. NOT Pattern D.

Net length: 776 lines (delta document; "UNCHANGED FROM v0.3"
references for unchanged sections).
