# 03 — Outreach Message Template

**Use:** Send to all 8-12 longlist candidates simultaneously. Personalize only the first line.

**Format:** Email is best. Phone is fine if email isn't listed. LinkedIn InMail is third choice. Avoid WhatsApp for first touch unless the candidate's website lists it explicitly as preferred.

**Tone:** Direct, respectful, specific. Architects are professionals; they will respect a clear ask and ignore vague ones.

---

## TEMPLATE A — First-touch email (preferred)

**Subject line options (pick one):**
- "Independent architectural review — single-family residential design software (Chennai)"
- "Paid review engagement — residential plan-generation system (6-10 hours)"
- "Audit consultation request: residential design rules + NBC 2016 / TNCDBR compliance"

---

```
Dear [Name],

I'm a Chennai-based founder building a decision-support tool that helps Indian families understand the architectural design of their own home before they break ground. The goal is to close the information gap between the homeowner and the architect/contractor — particularly around costs, regulatory compliance, and what the design decisions actually mean.

The product is software, not a building. It generates architectural plans for single-family residential plots (typically 1500-2400 sq ft, G+1, Tamil Nadu / NBC 2016 jurisdiction), with explicit rule-based logic for setbacks, FAR, room sizing, corridors, structural grid, hydraulics, doors, and a few other layers.

I'm at the v1 release-readiness stage. Before any real user touches this, I need an independent licensed architect to review the rules and design decisions embedded in the system — not the drawings themselves, but the LOGIC that produces drawings. I'm engaging an architect specifically to push back on choices I've made that may be wrong, out-of-date with NBC, or simply not how a practising architect would frame the problem.

Concretely, the engagement is:
- ~6-10 hours of your time, over 2-3 weeks
- Read 5-7 spec documents totaling ~80 pages (in English; I provide a reading order)
- Identify clauses, formulas, defaults, or framings you disagree with
- Deliver a written 2-4 page summary with specific findings I can act on

Compensation: ₹15,000 - ₹40,000 depending on scope and your usual rate.

I found you through [IIA TN chapter / Google search for "Chennai residential architect" / Anna University SAP alumni / your firm's website / mutual contact: NAME].

If you're potentially interested, would you be willing to answer 7 short vetting questions by email? They'll take you ~20 minutes and they help me pick a reviewer whose calibration matches the project's needs.

If not interested, no harm — a one-line "not for me" reply is appreciated so I know to look elsewhere.

Thanks for considering,
Ramalingam
[Phone] / [Email]
```

```

Glad to confirm I'd like to engage you for the review. Brief next steps:

1. I'll send a short engagement letter (1 page) capturing:
   - Scope (read the briefing pack, deliver written 2-4 page summary)
   - Compensation: ₹[NEGOTIATED AMOUNT], 50% advance via [bank/UPI/cheque], 50% on deliverable
   - Timeline: written deliverable within [N] days from receipt of briefing pack
   - IP: project documents are confidential; findings I receive remain mine; you may reference the engagement on your CV/portfolio at the level of "architectural review for residential software project" without identifying the product
   - Cancellation: either side may withdraw before milestone 1 with no fees; cancellation after milestone 1 = 50% payable

2. Once you confirm the engagement letter, I'll send:
   - The 10-document briefing pack (curated reading list with time estimates)
   - The structured review-question template (you fill in answers as you read)

3. Mid-review: I'd like a 60-minute call about halfway through to clarify framing if needed. Optional from your side.

4. End of engagement: written deliverable + optional 60-minute debrief call.

Does this work? If yes, I'll send the engagement letter today. If you want to adjust any term, let me know.

Best,
Ramalingam
```

## What to do AFTER the reply

| Reply type | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Yes, send the questions" | Send TEMPLATE C immediately. Track in a simple spreadsheet who's replied and when. |
| "Send me more details first" | Reply with the project summary (point to `00_README.md` content from this packet, simplified to one page). |
| Generic interest but no concrete answers | Send TEMPLATE C as gentle push toward the questions. |
| Counter-offer with much higher rate | Either accept (if budget allows) or decline politely (TEMPLATE E variant). |
| "I'd rather design something for you" | Decline; wrong fit. |
| No reply after TEMPLATE B follow-up | Drop, don't push further. |

**Estimate:** 30-50% of longlist will reply within 7-14 days. Of those who reply, 60-70% will answer the vetting questions. Of vetted candidates, 1-2 will be the right fit.
