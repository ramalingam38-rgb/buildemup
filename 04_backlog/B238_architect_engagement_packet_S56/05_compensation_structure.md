# 05 — Compensation Structure

**Budget anchor:** ₹15,000 - ₹40,000 total (from `S54_BACKLOG_TRIAGE.md` and `NEXT_CLAUDE_HANDOFF.md`).
**Payment-rail recommendation:** UPI for amounts under ₹50K is fast and traceable; bank NEFT also fine.
**Tax handling:** Architects above the GST threshold (₹20L turnover for services) will invoice with GST. Below threshold, simple invoice with PAN is sufficient. **Always insist on an invoice** so the expense is documentable.

---

## Recommended split

| Tier | Total budget | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| **Lean** | ₹15,000 | Junior independent architect, 5-10 years post-licensure, Tier-2 TN city, willing to do this for portfolio addition + reasonable rate |
| **Standard** | ₹25,000 - ₹30,000 | Mid-career independent architect, 10-15 years post-licensure, Chennai, active practice. Most likely landing spot. |
| **Premium** | ₹40,000 | Senior architect with current teaching/critic role, published author on Indian codes, gives extra weight to deliverable |

**Recommendation:** Aim for Standard tier (₹25K-₹30K). The Premium tier doesn't necessarily produce better findings — a working mid-career practitioner with current-day plan-approval exposure often catches more issues than a senior who's been out of approvals for years.

---

## Milestone structure (recommended)

| Milestone | % of total | When | What triggers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Advance** | 50% | At engagement letter signing | Architect commits calendar window; you commit budget |
| **Mid-checkpoint** | 0% (no payment) | Optional mid-review call | Just a calendar marker — no money moves |
| **Final** | 50% | On deliverable receipt + 1 follow-up question round | You're paying for the WRITTEN deliverable, not the time |

**Why this structure:**
- Advance signals seriousness on both sides; architects rarely book unsigned engagements.
- No mid-payment because the deliverable is what matters; if architect drops out mid-way, you've paid only for committed time.
- Final on deliverable + 1 follow-up means architect knows they have to write things down and answer one round of clarifications.

---

## Alternative — hourly engagement (NOT recommended)

Some architects may push for an hourly rate (e.g., ₹3,000/hour × estimated 8 hours = ₹24,000). This is workable but creates incentives to slow down or over-elaborate. **Prefer fixed-fee per deliverable.** If hourly is the only option, cap it explicitly: "₹3,000/hour, capped at ₹30,000 total regardless of hours."

---

## What MUST be in the engagement letter (in writing)

1. **Scope reference** — link/attach `04_scope_of_review.md` or summarize: "Review of 7 spec layers per attached scope document, single written 2-4 page deliverable."
2. **Fee amount** — exact total, exact split (e.g., ₹25,000 = ₹12,500 advance + ₹12,500 on deliverable).
3. **Payment method** — UPI handle / bank account details to be exchanged after signing.
4. **GST handling** — "Inclusive of GST if applicable" OR "Plus GST as applicable, against tax invoice." Pick one before signing.
5. **Timeline** — "Written deliverable due within N days from delivery of briefing pack." N = 14-21 days typical.
6. **IP clarity** — "All project documents (specs, code, KB rules, master doc) are property of Ramalingam and confidential. Architect's findings, once paid for, become Ramalingam's. Architect may reference the engagement on CV/portfolio at the level of 'architectural review for a residential design software project' without naming the product or its features."
7. **Cancellation terms** — "Either party may withdraw before advance payment is made with no fees. After advance payment, advance is forfeited; no further fees owed in either direction unless deliverable has been received."
8. **Liability clarification** — "Review is advisory only. Architect does not provide professional certification of the product. No third-party liability is created by this engagement."
9. **Names + dates + signatures** (digital signatures are fine for ₹15-40K range; paper signatures only if architect insists).

## What NOT to negotiate

- Don't haggle the architect's quoted rate below ₹15K unless they propose it. The point isn't lowest price; it's right reviewer.
- Don't agree to "I'll just review the drawings if you send them." That's not the scope. Hold the line.
- Don't agree to "I'll just give you verbal feedback." Hold the written-deliverable requirement.
- Don't agree to "I'd rather wait to see what comes back from CMDA before reviewing." That delays the review past calendar window. Either the architect can do the review on a spec or they can't.

## Indicative cost references (May 2026, Chennai)

| Service | Typical rate |
|---|---|
| Architect consultation, 1 hour, on existing drawing | ₹1,500 - ₹3,500 |
| Architect consultation, full day | ₹15,000 - ₹40,000 |
| Architectural plan-approval support (file preparation) | ₹15,000 - ₹50,000 per file |
| Mid-career independent architect, retainer | ₹50,000 - ₹1,50,000/month |
| Senior architect with teaching role, project review | ₹40,000 - ₹1,00,000 per review |

The B-238 engagement sits at the upper end of "full day consultation" rates — that's appropriate for the scope. Anything below ₹15K signals a skim review; anything above ₹50K is over-scoped for what we're asking.
